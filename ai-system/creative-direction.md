## 3. ART DIRECTION

The website should feel like a boutique premium healthcare brand with calm editorial elegance rather than a generic SaaS or template-based dental website.

Prioritize:

- restraint
- whitespace
- visual calmness
- sophistication
- cinematic composition
- premium healthcare trust

## 4. VISUAL RHYTHM

Improve visual rhythm across the homepage.

Avoid repetitive centered section structures and identical card grids stacked consecutively.

Introduce subtle variation through:

- spacing
- alignment
- composition
- layout pacing
- section density
while preserving consistency.

## 5. SIGNATURE MOTIF

Create a subtle signature visual motif derived from the brand identity and apply it consistently throughout the homepage.

Use it as:

- background accents
- section dividers
- decorative shapes
- image framing
- subtle UI details

Ensure the motif feels elegant and healthcare-premium rather than decorative for its own sake.

# FINAL CREATIVE GOAL

The website should feel:

- thoughtfully designed
